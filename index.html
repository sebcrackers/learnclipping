Clipping Is the New Gold Rush — And the Pickaxe Is Free

Every day, thousands of hours of long-form content get uploaded to YouTube, Twitch, and podcasts. Buried inside those hours are thirty-second moments — a hot take, a joke, a controversial opinion — that millions of people would watch, share, and engage with. The people who find those moments and turn them into vertical videos are called clippers. Some of them make hundreds. Some make thousands. A few make six figures a month.

The barrier to entry is almost zero. You need a computer, free software, and an eye for what's entertaining. You don't need a camera. You don't need to be on video. You don't need a following to start. You're not building a personal brand — you're building a content machine that works while you sleep.

This publication exists to teach you exactly how that machine works, from the first clip you cut to the first dollar you deposit. We'll cover the tools, the workflow, the platforms that pay, and the common mistakes that keep beginners stuck at zero. Turn the page. Let's get to work.
